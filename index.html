Grounding Supply Chain Agents in Trusted Context

Global supply chains face constant disruption, and traditional automation can't keep pace. The real value isn't just deploying AI agents, it's grounding them in trusted context so they can act with confidence and governance. Discover how to build a trusted data foundation so your agents stop guessing and start delivering outcomes you can stand behind.

Join Ary Paydawy, Community of Practice Leader at Informatica from Salesforce, for a session that sets the context on agentic AI and trusted data, then walks through live demos showing how headless MDM powers AI agents to act with confidence, including identifying and onboarding an alternative vendor during a product recall, right inside Slack.

You'll learn how to:

  • Ground agentic AI in AI-ready data and trusted context
  • Use headless MDM to unify transactional, supplier, and compliance data
  • Maintain decision governance and auditable outcomes as agents take action
